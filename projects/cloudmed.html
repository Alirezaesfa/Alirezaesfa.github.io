CloudMed: one cloud viewer for medical imaging

Doctors read assigned images in one web viewer, and patients see reports from all their doctors in one place.

Role
Co-founder
When
2019 to 2020
Where
Tehran
Outcome
Funded twice, then pivoted
+40%client acquisition from product demos

What I did

  • Gathered requirements from imaging centers, doctors and patients, wrote the user stories, and made sure Ministry of Health requirements were met.
  • Benchmarked 11 cloud imaging vendors on features and pricing models (per storage, per study, packages) and recommended the roadmap: a radiology information system first to reach parity, then data analytics to stand out.
  • Ran product demos that grew client acquisition by 40%, and wrote the business plan.

Result

We raised funding twice, from an angel investor and then a VC package after placing 2nd in the 100Startups competition ($200K+). The product later pivoted into an educational app for doctors that now serves thousands of users.
