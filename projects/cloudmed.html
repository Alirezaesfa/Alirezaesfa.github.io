Startup · Co-founder · 2019 to 2020

CloudMed: one cloud viewer for medical imaging

The idea: doctors read assigned images in one web viewer, and patients see reports from all their doctors in one place.

Role
Co-founder
When
2019 to 2020
Where
Tehran
Outcome
Two funding rounds, then a pivot I led
10,000+users reached by the doctors' app that came out of the pivot I led

What I did

  • Ran discovery with imaging centers, doctors, patients and administrators, and turned their needs and Ministry of Health privacy rules into epics and user stories with acceptance criteria.
  • Made sure pilot deployments met those rules (encryption, role-based access, audit logging), working with legal and IT.
  • Designed the MySQL database for patient records and imaging data received through APIs, cutting data entry errors by 25%.
  • Benchmarked 11 cloud imaging vendors on features and pricing models (per storage, per study, packages) and recommended the roadmap: a radiology information system first to reach parity, then data analytics to stand out.
  • Ran product demos that grew client acquisition by 40%, and wrote the business plan.

Result

We raised two funding rounds of about $200K+ each: from an angel investor, then a VC package after placing 2nd in the 100Startups competition. When the imaging product wasn't generating revenue, I ran a customer-centric market analysis, proposed the pivot to an educational social media app for doctors, and built its foundation before I left. The app went on to reach 10,000+ users and about $100K CAD in annual revenue.
