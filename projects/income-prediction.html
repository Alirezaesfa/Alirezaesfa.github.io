Income prediction for customer targeting

Knowing which customers are likely to earn over $50K helps a business, like a premium insurer, target the right people. Two projects on the same Census data, two toolsets.

Role
Team member
When
During my MSc
Where
Concordia
Data
US Census

In Python (team of 3)

  • Built and compared seven classifiers on 32,561 records and 14 features: logistic regression, KNN, Gaussian naive Bayes, decision tree, SVM, a deep neural network and an autoencoder.
  • Ran feature selection by variance and retrained each model; the decision tree fit best, and the reduced-feature version was recommended because it needs less compute.

In SAS Enterprise Miner

  • Cleaned the 48,842-record dataset (missing-value markers, redundant variables) and compared five models, choosing the decision tree with the lowest misclassification rate.
  • Identified the strongest predictors: marital status, capital gains and losses, occupation, education, hours worked and age.
