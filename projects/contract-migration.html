AI contract review for a 12K-contract migration

About 60K accounts and 12K contracts had to move to a new platform after an acquisition, and every contract needed a privacy and data-residency review first. An AI team from another organization built the reviewer; I was the business analyst between that team, the business, Legal and the Data Trust Office.

The problem

Every contract carried data-residency and privacy risk, and reviewing 12K of them by hand was not realistic. The AI review had to meet requirements from the business, Legal and the Data Trust Office before anything moved.

What I did

  • Wrote the business requirements document (BRD) with functional and non-functional requirements, turning privacy and data-residency rules from Legal and the Data Trust Office into testable acceptance criteria.
  • Built Python scripts that pull account data from Salesforce and move the matching contracts into Google Drive for the review, and removed a manual download step from every run.
  • Ran prototyping and validation with the AI team, Legal and the Data Trust Office: the AI team refined the model on their feedback until its risk summaries matched their review expectations.
  • Ran user acceptance testing one contract language at a time, six in total: batches of contracts, business and legal reviewers checking the AI's risk summaries against the acceptance criteria, fixes requested from the AI team, and retests until each language passed.
  • Handed the finished solution over to the business as a self-serve tool, so teams now run their own contract reviews.

Result

Three phases are live, and the program team delivered about $2M in benefit. Within it, I identified a $408K piece: 2025 benefits that had gone unclaimed. I helped build the business case, and senior leadership signed it off. The solution is now a reusable self-serve capability; one team has used about 7,000 of 8,500 AI outputs.
