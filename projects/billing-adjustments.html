AI recommendations for billing adjustment reviews

Validators review every billing adjustment request by hand, pulling information from billing and contracting systems and internal privacy documents, so reviews are slow and the backlog is large.

What I did

  • Mapped how requests are created, reviewed and decided, with the subject matter experts who do the work.
  • Analyzed the 2025 request data in Excel and picked the top 5 request types, by impact, for the proof of concept.
  • Wrote the process design document for the future state, and now coordinate the subject matter experts and the AI team.

Status

The AI team is training the model on real use cases from subject matter experts. The goal is to help validators review faster by giving them a recommendation built from the data they would otherwise look up by hand. Validators will keep making every decision. The work is in progress, so there are no results to report yet.

Work details are generalized, and the simulation uses simulated data.
