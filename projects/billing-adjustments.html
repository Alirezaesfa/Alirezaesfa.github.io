AI recommendations for billing adjustment reviews

Sales submits billing adjustment requests, but more than 80% arrive without enough information. Validators spend about 45 minutes on each one, pulling data from billing and contracting systems and checking internal policies, and the backlog is large. An interim AI model is now live: it recommends a decision on each request, and reviews are much faster.

Where

What I did

  • Mapped how requests are submitted, reviewed and decided, with the subject matter experts who do the work.
  • Analyzed the 2025 request data in Excel and picked the top 5 request types, by impact, to start with.
  • Wrote the process design document for the future state, and coordinate the subject matter experts and the AI team.
  • As acting PM, took the review side to a live interim model in production with the AI team and the validators.

The solution

  • For validators (live): the AI queries the billing and contracting systems, reads the company policies and checks past requests from that customer, then gives an AI recommendation. Validators can add supporting information, then approve or reject. People keep every decision.
  • For sales (next): an AI agent connected to the billing and contracting systems and the internal policies. Sales describes the request, and the agent creates a complete request in Salesforce for them, so they no longer need to ask another team to submit it on their behalf.

Status

The interim model is live in production. The validator now reviews with it: its recommendations are 95% accurate, and reviews are much faster. The next step is the sales-facing agent, so requests arrive complete.
