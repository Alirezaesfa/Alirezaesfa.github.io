TELUS · Billing operations · 2026

AI recommendations for billing adjustment reviews

Sales submits billing adjustment requests, but more than 80% arrive without enough information. Validators spend about 45 minutes on each one, pulling data from billing and contracting systems and checking internal policies, and the backlog is large.

Role
Business analyst, acting PM
When
2026
Where
TELUS, B2B AI
Status
In progress
80%+of requests arrive without enough information, the problem this redesign tackles

What I did

  • Mapped how requests are submitted, reviewed and decided, with the subject matter experts who do the work.
  • Analyzed the 2025 request data in Excel and picked the top 5 request types, by impact, to start with.
  • Wrote the process design document for the future state, and now coordinate the subject matter experts and the AI team.

The solution being built

  • For sales: a chatbot connected to the billing and contracting systems and the internal policies. Sales describes the request, and the bot reads that customer's information and writes the request into Salesforce.
  • For validators: a review queue where they can add supporting information to get an AI recommendation, then approve or reject. People keep every decision.

Status

The AI team is training the model on real use cases from subject matter experts. The work is in progress, so there are no results to report yet.

Work details are generalized, and the simulation uses simulated data.
