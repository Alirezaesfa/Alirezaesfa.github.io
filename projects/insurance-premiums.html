MSc project · 2022

What drives life insurance premiums

For a new North American insurer: which customer factors actually drive the yearly premium, and which only look like they do?

Role
Team member, team of 4
When
2022
Where
MSc, Concordia
Tool
SPSS

What we did

  • Built multiple and stepwise regression models of the yearly premium.
  • Detected outliers and checked the assumptions, including multicollinearity (VIF) and constant variance, and transformed BMI where it violated an assumption.

Result

Age, number of children, smoking and BMI drove premiums; sex was not significant in any model.
