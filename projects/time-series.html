MSc project · 2023

Time-series forecasting with deep learning

Five time series of about 1,400 observations each, and six models compared on how well they forecast data they haven't seen.

Role
Builder
When
2023
Where
MSc, Concordia
Data
5 series

What I did

  • Scaled each series and held out test data for evaluation.
  • Trained KNN regression (scikit-learn) and LSTM, bidirectional LSTM, GRU, bidirectional GRU and 1D CNN models (Keras) for every series.
  • Evaluated each model on held-out data with RMSE, MAE and MAPE and compared predicted against actual values.
