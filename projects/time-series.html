Time-series forecasting with deep learning

Five time series of about 1,400 observations each, and one question: which model forecasts them best on data it hasn't seen?

PythonKerasscikit-learnLSTMGRUCNNRMSE / MAE / MAPE
Role
Builder
When
2023
Where
MSc, Concordia
Data
5 series

What I did

  • Scaled each series and held out test data for evaluation.
  • Trained KNN regression, LSTM, bidirectional LSTM, GRU, bidirectional GRU and a 1D CNN in Keras for every series.
  • Evaluated each model on held-out data with RMSE, MAE and MAPE and compared predicted against actual values.
