About

From engineering to AI delivery

I co-founded a health-tech startup and raised funding twice, then worked as a business analyst at a trading company, gathering requirements for a new inventory system.

In 2022 I started an MSc at Concordia in Montreal, with data mining and machine learning courses and a qualitative thesis. In January 2026 I joined TELUS as a business analyst in process intelligence, working on AI and automation from requirements to adoption.
